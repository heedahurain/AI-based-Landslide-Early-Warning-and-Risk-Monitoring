# ml/

The four-layer risk engine, its training pipelines and the model registry.

## What lands here, and when

**Phase 3** delivers all four layers:

- `physics/` — infinite-slope factor of safety with transient pore pressure from
  a simplified Iverson / Green–Ampt infiltration response. Parameters come from
  the database-backed `geotech_parameter` table, editable by the geologist role.
  This layer has no training data and cannot overfit; it is the defensible floor
  of the system.
- `empirical/` — regional rainfall intensity–duration thresholds with bootstrap
  confidence bands, the antecedent precipitation index, cumulative rainfall
  windows and a tank-model soil water index.
- `models/` — the susceptibility ensemble (XGBoost and LightGBM) and the
  temporal hazard network (a PyTorch temporal convolutional network), with
  focal loss, isotonic calibration and MC-dropout uncertainty.
- `deformation/` — Sentinel-1 backscatter and coherence change, plus the
  inverse-velocity (Fukuzono) time-of-failure estimator with an R² quality gate.
- `explain/` — SHAP explanation assembled into the payload the UI drawer renders.

## Two rules that are not negotiable

1. **Evaluate with leave-one-district-out spatial cross-validation.** Random
   k-fold leaks spatially, because neighbouring slope units share terrain,
   lithology and the same rainfall field. It inflates AUC and produces a model
   that fails in the district you have not seen, which is the operational case.
2. **Never invent a metric.** Print what you measure and record the MLflow run
   identifier next to it. `../docs/MODEL_CARD.md` has empty tables on purpose
   until a real evaluation fills them.

## The inventory caveat

Until a real GSI or COOLR landslide inventory is obtained, training labels are
generated by running the physics model over the synthetic history. Metrics from
that data measure whether the ML layer learned the generating process, not
whether it predicts real landslides, and must be reported as
**synthetic-inventory validation**. See `../docs/MODEL_CARD.md` §5.2.
